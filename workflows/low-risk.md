# L0 — low risk

Use for documentation, typo fixes, fixtures, clear mechanical maintenance, and bounded non-behavioural refactors.

1. Confirm scope and non-goals in the Issue or PR.
2. Make the smallest change.
3. Run the relevant validator, formatter, or focused test.
4. Open a PR with the actual command/result and any skipped check.
5. Human reviews and merges.

Escalate to L1 when behaviour, an external contract, ambiguity, or meaningful regression risk appears. Escalate to L2 for security, data, production, migration, or irreversible risk.
